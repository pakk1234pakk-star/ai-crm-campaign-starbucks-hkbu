# Day 4 Cross-Functional Agile Recovery Plan

**Campaign:** Starbucks × HKBU – “Brewing Balance: From EMO to Wellness”

## 1. Immediate Demand-Throttling Actions (Physical + Digital)

- Cap walk-in orders at the podium booth to 8 drinks every 15 minutes.
- Activate a digital virtual queue in the Starbucks Rewards HK App (estimated wait shown in real time).
- Redirect overflow customers to the nearest permanent Starbucks store via in-app map + “Skip the Queue” 10-star bonus if they order Mobile Order & Pay before 15:00.
- Deploy temporary shade and water stations at the booth to reduce heat-related complaints.

## 2. App-Driven Recovery Mechanics (IF / THEN / ELSE)

```
IF current MOP queue length > 15 orders  
THEN  
  - Automatically open a Gold/Staff priority production lane  
  - Push notification to Gold members: “Your priority lane is now active – order now for pickup in <8 min”  
ELSE  
  - Keep normal dual-lane production  

IF customer is Gold tier OR Staff/Lecturer verified  
THEN  
  - Allow one free size-upgrade or oat-milk upgrade once per day during the crisis window  
ELSE  
  - Standard menu pricing applies  

IF virtual-queue wait estimate > 25 minutes  
THEN  
  - Offer an automatic off-peak voucher (valid 16:00–18:00 or after 19:00) worth HK$15 off any handcrafted drink  
ELSE  
  - No extra incentive  
```

## 3. Product Fix / Alternative Offer

- Immediately pull the Zen Chamomile Oat Latte from the booth menu.
- Replace it with a limited “Calm Focus Latte” (existing oat-milk + mild floral syrup recipe).
- For any customer who already purchased the defective drink: issue an automatic 25-star compensation + free drink voucher via the App.
- Train booth baristas on the new recipe overnight; print new temporary menu cards.

## 4. Evening PT Inclusion Package (after 18:00)

- Extend booth operating hours until 20:30 on Day 4–7.
- Launch “Night Owl Power Bundle” visible only after 18:00 in the App:
  - Any handcrafted drink + pastry for HK$58
  - Exclusive 15 Bonus Stars for evening PT students who show student ID + App profile.
- Push notification at 17:45 to evening-segment users.
